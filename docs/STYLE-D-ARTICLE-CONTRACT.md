# Style D — Daily Structure Article Authority

This document is the style-specific human authority for D. It governs a D1
structure narrative, technical support/resistance levels, conditional
scenarios, invalidation, and the supporting weekly calendar context.

The weekly calendar is supporting evidence only. A calendar-only artifact does
not authorize scoring. Article-bound technical facts and the validator result
must be present in the evidence envelope.
