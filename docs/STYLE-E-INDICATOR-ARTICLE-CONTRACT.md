# Style E — Indicator Article Authority

This document is the style-specific human authority for E. Inputs are H1 and
M15: M15 is a source/decision input, not an implied visual requirement.

The current visual manifest requires exactly two H1 roles: `h1_fibonacci` and
`h1_trade_plan`. An M15-only image cannot satisfy either role. Indicator facts,
trade-plan QA, plan status, and validator evidence remain article-bound.
